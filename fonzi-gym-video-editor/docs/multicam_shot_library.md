## Flow prompt template (one per shot)

"Hard cut to [SHOT DESCRIPTION, e.g. 'Low angle looking up, chest up']. Preserve identity and room exactly: same person, same face, same clothes, same gym, same lighting. Keep the original voice, speech and lip-sync exactly — do not rewrite, drop or add words. No music, no subtitles, no text on screen. Vertical 9:16, 8 seconds."

## Rules

- Every shot is a HARD CUT. No morph, no dissolve, no slow transition, no camera move between angles
  unless the shot itself says so.
- Same person, face, body, clothes, tattoos, gym, equipment and lighting. Nothing added or removed.
- Anchor: the frame from Alfonso's REAL video at the shot's timestamp.
- Original audio is the truth: no dropped or rewritten words, no new music, no subtitles, no text.
- 4 shots per video, from 4 DIFFERENT categories, fitted to the moment (hard rep: low hero / detail
  close-up; talking: face / chest up; setup: wide / full body). No shot number repeats from the previous
  video unless Alfonso asks. Shot 100 only as the closing angle. Shots that cannot be done safely or
  cleanly in Flow (e.g. 74 camera on the bar, 42 fisheye) are skipped for the next best from another category.
